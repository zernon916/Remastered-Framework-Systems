---------------------------------------
Beacons are the computer source. Wire a Battery container (electricity).
Optional logic switch as on/off. Unpowered or out of range → tethered bots revert.

  Hack Beacon (red)       16 m  tether only — Hideout: 20 Farmers
  Control Beacon          32 m  faster tether — Hideout: 50 Farmers
  Infection Beacon        48 m  can permanently submit — Hideout: 120 Farmers

All three are Hideout item trades (not schematics) when RFS Beacons B&P is enabled.
Craftbot still has Hack Beacon (1 Metal placeholder). Control / Infection stay hideout-only.

Tethered bots can hop to another powered beacon in range if theirs dies.
Infected bots no longer need a beacon. /hijack cheat infects immediately.

Phase 6 — MiniMap (HUD) [FUTURE]
--------------------------------
Status: PLANNED, NOT IN CURRENT BUILD — do not implement or copy yet.

Preferred path (after permission from Nutt, author of Steam Workshop

Fallback (if permission or code cannot ship): original RFS HUD schematic
minimap as already documented below — study Workshop 3780282057
approach only; do not port their scripts, layouts, tile-photo atlas,
GPS tool, or assets into this pack.

What they did vs what we have (approach summary, no copy until permission):
  World Map: always-on circular HUD overlay (non-interactive jsonGui) in a
  screen corner. Terrain cells (~64 m) from client terrain data; a packed
  aerial-photo atlas for known vanilla tiles; biome-color + road hint when
  a tile has no photo. Player stays in first-person; the ring scrolls with
  world position each frame; cell pictures refill only when you cross a
  cell. Facing arrow from camera heading. Held GPS opens a fullscreen
  atlas map (pan/zoom, click waypoint, compass pin). Hide the ring when
  another GUI/chat is up (mods cannot draw under chat). No exploration fog.
  RFS today: `/map` is a live top-down cutscene camera (WASD pan, scroll
  zoom, character locked/immovable; lock-part + Player.lua camera backup).
  `/menu` Map only opens that camera. Always-on HUD (`RfsHud`) is clock +
  compass text — not a map. No tile atlas, no HUD ring, no map pins for
  player / beacons / allies.

Roadmap (HUD toggle, markers, keep `/map` — same either path):
  - HUD minimap, always-on or toggle (`/menu` and/or chat). Corner overlay
    so the player can walk while it updates. Square/rounded layout widget
    is enough for v1 (true circular clip is engine-awkward).
  - Preferred path (permission granted): incorporate Nutt's minimap + map.
    Credit Nutt in Steam / description.json and in-game/docs when this
    ships (not before). Fallback: draw our own map — schematic
    biome/grid from terrain cell data (and/or later original RFS art).
    Never reuse 3780282057 photos, sheets, or GUI unless permission is
    confirmed.
  - Refine existing `/map` as the full map (live camera). Optional: click
    or `/menu` Map from the HUD to open it. Keep lock/fallback polish.
    Preferred path may fold in Nutt's map; fallback does not replace
    `/map` with someone else's atlas screen.
  - Markers: player (heading), powered beacons, allies. Letters later
    (H1/T2-style, after Phase 2 naming / Orders badges).
  - Toggle + size/corner prefs may later share the Phase 3.6 GUI tab.
  - No GPS item required (chat `/map` already exists). Optional gadget later.
  - Fog of war: not in the studied mod (missing tiles = flat color). Skip
    for v1, or add original visited-cell fog later — not a port.
  - Waypoints / compass pin: later polish, not v1.

Feasibility (from the study, for our own code):
  - HUD overlay is already proven (`RfsHud` layout, isHud, non-interactive).
    Prefer Player.lua + layout like the clock, not a Workshop autoTool.
  - `sm.storage.loadTerrainData` can drive a schematic cell grid without
    photographs. Live 3D cannot be an inset HUD widget (camera is global).
  - Hide the HUD map while chat/other GUIs are open (same layering limit).
  - Update: follow world pos every frame for pan; refill cells on cell
    change only. MP: keep init on the local player (RfsHud already does).
  - Ally/beacon pins need positions we already track in hijack/beacon code;
    do not copy another mod's marker widgets.

Phase 6 does not block factories or Orders.
