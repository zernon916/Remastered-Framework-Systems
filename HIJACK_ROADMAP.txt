Recipe Framework Survival — Robot hijack roadmap
================================================
Author: Zernon916
Home: RFS Custom Game (wraps Survival RobotSelectTarget).
Intelligentia is optional (turret skip + later craftable tiers). Original RFS only.

Phase 1 (SHIPPED)
-----------------
- /hijack [range] — convert nearest hostile robot (cheat = permanent infect)
- /hijacklist — tethered vs infected counts
- /givehack — Hack / Control / Infection Beacons
- Allies ignore players and attack other robots
- Hostiles can peel onto nearby allies (defense)
- Green ally / deeper green infected
- Persist playerAlly on the unit's own saved table (reload of that unit)
- _G.g_rfsIsPlayerAlly(unit) for Intelligentia

Beacon computer (author idea — SHIPPED)
---------------------------------------
Beacons are the computer source. Wire a Battery container (electricity).
Optional logic switch as on/off. Unpowered or out of range → tethered bots revert.

  Hack Beacon (red)       16 m  tether only
    Craftbot: 1 Metal (placeholder). Hideout: 20 Farmers (item, not schematic).
  Control / Infection beacons: planned later (not in shop yet).

Tethered bots can hop to another powered beacon in range if theirs dies.
Infected bots no longer need a beacon. /hijack cheat infects immediately.

NOT in Phase 1: chain convert, harvest/replant/chest, factories, identities,
per-type farm roles.

Phase 2 — Identity + chain (mid)
--------------------------------
- Named identities after hijack (storage: owner, name, type, first-seen tick)
- Optional nametag overlay (same billboard idea as Growth Time)
- Chain convert: ally bots in radius convert hostiles (slow, chance, or on melee)
- Intelligentia official skip of g_rfsIsPlayerAlly

Phase 3 — Orders (hard)
-----------------------
Vanilla tote/hay/farm do NOT plant, plow, or use chests. Crop targeting is grief.
New job states on allies only:
  follow / stay / guard
  harvest (player-loot rules + carry buffer — units have no inventory)
  replant on soil
  deposit nearest chest
Orders via hack beacon GUI (like /setup) or chat /botorder
Per-type flavor (author list):
  totebot  — plants, hits stronger enemies, poor base defense
  haybot   — plows, hits weaker enemies, good defense
  tapebot  — ranged sentry
  farmbot  — tank / late escort

Phase 4 — Factories (late game / Wonk)
--------------------------------------
- Craftable factory interactable (Intelligentia or RFS part)
- Spend resources to sm.unit.createUnit(uuid, pos, yaw, { playerAlly=true, color=..., tetherPoint=... })
- Capsules stay hostile (vanilla); factory is the ally spawner
- Caps on army size
- Optional: tapebot assembly line, haybot "barn", farmbot "garage"

Phase 5 — Polish
